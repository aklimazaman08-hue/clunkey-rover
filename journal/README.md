# Development Journal

This folder documents the development of the Clunkey Rover: Mini Autonomous Robot. The journal records design decisions, project progress, testing plans, challenges, and lessons learned throughout the project.

## What the Journal Tracks

- Initial rover concept and sketches
- CAD and chassis design decisions
- Electronics planning
- Firmware and autonomous behavior planning
- Parts and budget decisions
- Problems encountered during development
- Changes made to the design
- Lessons learned from the project

## Project Development

The Clunkey Rover is being designed as a small four-wheel-drive autonomous rover inspired by FIRST Robotics, mechanical engineering, and robotics. The project is being developed in stages, starting with the concept and system design before moving toward CAD, electronics, firmware, and physical construction.

## Current Status

The current stage focuses on completing the design documentation, CAD planning, electronics architecture, firmware planning, and bill of materials before beginning the physical build.

## Future Journal Entries

Future entries will document the physical build, wiring, programming, testing, troubleshooting, and improvements made to the rover.
